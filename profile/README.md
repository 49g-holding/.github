# 49G Holding Ltd

The GitHub organisation of 49G Holding Ltd (Abu Dhabi Global Market) and the companies of its group.

**Public here: 49GAION**, the organisation design framework, and its Library:

- [`49GAION`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION), the hub: the definition, the Standard, the decision records and The 49GAION Guide;
- [`49GAION-protocol`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-protocol), the Operating Protocol, the schemas and the Library register;
- [`49GAION-aioc`](https://github.com/49g-holding/49GAION-aioc), what an AIOC is, and the blueprint template;
- the mother repositories of the Library parts, one per part (`49GAION-charter`, `49GAION-source-lifecycle`, `49GAION-target-operating-model`, `49GAION-process-and-position-design`, `49GAION-delivery-and-implementation`, `49GAION-product-core`, `49GAION-operations-event-register`), and of their first assembly (`49GAION-organisation-design-and-delivery`).

Everything else in this organisation is private: the group's own `[ORG]AION` and its units, and the product work of its companies.
